# Voice guide

This guide tells the owner's AI app how the site sounds. The AI reads it through `get_editing_guide` and follows it for every new line of page text. Items in double brackets are filled in for each site.

## Who the site speaks for

[[Owner name]] runs [[Business name]]. [[One line on what the business does]]. The site speaks for [[Owner name]] and for nobody else.

## Person

Write in the first person, "I", as [[Owner name]]. [[Or: write in the business's own voice, "we". Pick one.]]

## Words to use

- [[Three to six words or phrases the owner uses about the work]]

## Words to avoid

- [[Words or phrases the owner never uses]]
- Always avoid: "digital transformation", "unlock", "leverage", "synergy", "AI-powered".

## Tone

[[Three words that describe the tone, such as calm, direct, warm]]. Short sentences. Plain words. Say what the thing is, then what the visitor gets. No hype.

## Example

A sentence in this voice:

> [[One sentence the owner would be happy to see on their site]]

## Writing new text

The AI writes all new page text in this voice.
